Common Questions

Do treatments hurt? +

Most treatments are comfortable. We use topical numbing cream and cooling technology where needed. You can request to stop at any time.

How long until I see results? +

Facials show immediate glow. Laser treatments, injectables, and body contouring build results over 2-8 weeks as your body responds.

Is there downtime? +

Most clients return to work the same day. Each treatment menu lists specific downtime. We'll discuss this during your consultation.
