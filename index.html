Kind words

Questions, answered

Do treatments hurt?

Most feel mild. We use numbing cream and cooling where needed, and you can stop at any time.

How long until I see results?

Facials show an instant glow. Laser, injectables and body treatments build over two to eight weeks.

Is there downtime?

Each treatment lists its downtime in the menu. Most clients return to work the same day.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your visit.
